01 / WHO WE ARE

Technology with
analytical discipline.

NorthByte Norway Tec Ltd. is an independent civilian technology, cybersecurity and intelligence research company. Its work combines defensive security, open-source research, digital risk intelligence, protective security, strategic analysis and interactive technology.

Activities are conducted through publicly available information, legally accessible sources and explicitly authorized security research. NorthByte is not a government, military, intelligence or law-enforcement organization.

02 / FOUNDER & CEO

Doğukan Yegin.

Doğukan Yegin is the CEO of NorthByte Norway Tec Ltd., working across cybersecurity, open-source intelligence, protective security and technology. His professional background also includes system security and help-desk work, software and web development, public relations, media and R&D-oriented training.

At NorthByte, he brings these disciplines together around a defensive and evidence-based approach: research, verification, digital resilience, training and technology development.
